01 Special Containment Procedures

SCP-8374 is stored in a locked, windowless containment chamber at Site-██. Access requires authorization from designated staff.

  • Personnel must not wear SCP-8374.
  • Handling is restricted to authorized staff.
  • Unusual visual experiences must be reported immediately.
  • Testing requires approval from the project lead.
  • The object must remain under observation during approved tests.
WARNING: Never wear or modify the costume. These procedures are part of a fictional story.

02 Description

SCP-8374 is a brown teddy bear mascot costume of unknown origin. Its eyes remain open regardless of the costume's position.

When worn, it causes increasing tiredness and vivid, dreamlike visual experiences while the wearer is awake.

The wearer may see figures that appear real and seem capable of touching or affecting them. Nearby observers cannot see these figures.

Wearers also report a burning sensation around the eyes and difficulty blinking normally. The cause remains unknown.

RESEARCH NOTE 8374-D

The reported phenomena are currently unexplained within the fictional case file.
