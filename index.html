Item #: SCP-8374

THE IMAGINARY TEDDY BEAR

OBJECT CLASSEuclid
THREAT LEVELElevated
STATUSContained

ARTIST'S IMPRESSION — SCP-8374

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-8374 must be stored in a locked containment locker when not under authorized observation. No person may wear the costume without written approval from the research director. Testing must stop immediately if a subject reports seeing unfamiliar figures or feeling unexplained physical contact.

Description

SCP-8374 is a brown teddy bear mascot costume with permanently open eyes. The costume appears ordinary when unoccupied. Its anomalous effects begin when a person wears it.

After an initial period, the wearer experiences increasing fatigue and vivid hallucinations while remaining awake. Figures seen by the wearer may interact with them physically. Reported effects include unexplained impacts and injuries despite no visible source.

Wearers also report intense burning sensations in their eyes. They describe difficulty blinking normally, as if the costume's permanently open eyes affect their own perception.

Discovery

SCP-8374 came to Foundation attention after an incident in which a person wearing the costume claimed that imaginary creatures were chasing and attacking them. Witnesses could not see the creatures, but the wearer showed signs of having experienced physical impacts.

Interview Log 8374-A

Researcher: You understand that the figures may not be visible to anyone else?

“They're imaginary. I know that. But they can touch me.”

Researcher: Why do you think you cannot escape them?

“Because you can't see them.”

Current Status

SCP-8374 remains contained. Further testing requires approval.
